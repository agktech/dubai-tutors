Questions parents ask

Do tutors on this app need a permit?

Yes. In the UAE, private tutors need a permit to teach. Tutors who hold one show a “Permit verified” badge. Tutors who are applying show that status on their card.

How do I pay the tutor?

Directly to the tutor — most accept bank transfer or cash. Agree the price and payment method with the tutor on WhatsApp before the first lesson. Dubai Tutors does not take payment.

Can I try one lesson first?

Yes. Most parents start with a single trial lesson and then decide. Ask the tutor about a trial when you message them.

Home visit or online — which is better?

Home visits suit younger children. Online suits IGCSE and A-Level students who are used to studying on a screen. Use the “Lesson mode” filter to see tutors for each.

What if the tutor is not a good fit?

Just tell the tutor after the trial lesson. You are free to try another tutor from the list — there is no booking fee.

How do you stop fake tutors?

Every tutor registration is reviewed by our team before it appears on this page. Tutors must show permit proof to earn the “Permit verified” badge. If a tutor misbehaves, use the “Report this tutor” link on their card and we will remove them.

Should I pay in advance?

No. Never pay in advance. Start with one trial lesson, and pay the tutor only after it. If anyone asks for advance payment, report them using the “Report this tutor” link on their card.
